# Indefine LMS — Employee Guide

A short, practical guide to using the learning portal: signing in, watching
training videos, passing quizzes, and tracking your score.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the portal link your admin shared (the Railway URL, e.g. `https://…up.railway.app`).
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
3. Use your **work Microsoft 365 account** — the same one you use for Outlook/Teams.
   There is no separate password to remember.

That's it. The first time you sign in, your account is created automatically.

## 2. Your dashboard

After signing in you land on your **dashboard**. From top to bottom:

- **Growth Wizard** banner — a 5-minute setup for your year's goals (see §7).
- **Trajectory** card — three rings (Mastery, Delivery, Growth), your **tier**, and
  your **total score out of 100**, plus six track tiles.
- **Welcome** card — your streak, total points, rank, videos watched, and badges.
- **Attendance & daily punch** — quick links to **greytHR** (punch in/out) and **Turia**.
- **Your courses** — the training modules assigned to you.

> 📷 Screenshot: the full dashboard after sign-in.

Top-right buttons: **Recap** (your year-in-review), **Initiatives**, **Leaderboard**,
and **Sign out**.

## 3. Watching a training video

1. On the dashboard, click a course card or a video in the list.
2. The video plays in the main panel. The list on the right shows every video in the
   module and your progress through each.
3. **Playback speed** — use the **Speed** buttons under the player (0.5× up to 2×) to
   watch faster or slower. The selected speed is highlighted.
4. Your progress is saved automatically as you watch. The bar under the player shows
   the **% watched**.

> 📷 Screenshot: a video playing, with the speed buttons and the module list on the right.

**Important:** watching faster (2×) is fine — you still have to watch the whole video
to complete it. Skipping ahead does **not** count as watched.

---

## 4. Taking a quiz

Most videos have a quiz that **unlocks once you've watched ~90%** of the video.

1. When the quiz unlocks, the **Start quiz** button appears under the video.
2. Read the rules shown (time limit, pass mark, attempts allowed).
3. Click **Start quiz**. A timer appears at the top — it turns red in the final 30 seconds.
4. Answer each multiple-choice question, then click **Submit** (or it auto-submits when
   the timer runs out).
5. You'll see your **result** immediately. If you didn't pass, use **Retake** to try again.

> 📷 Screenshot: the quiz player with the timer and a question.
>
> 📷 Screenshot: the result screen showing the score and Retake button.

Your **best** score on each quiz is what counts toward your total — so retaking only
helps you.

## 5. The leaderboard

Click **Leaderboard** (top-right of the dashboard).

- The **top 3** appear on a podium; everyone else is listed below.
- Filter by **branch** or **department** using the dropdowns.
- Each row shows your points broken down: **Quiz · Deadline · Assignment · Attendance**.
- Switch to the **Branches** tab to compare branches.

> 📷 Screenshot: the leaderboard podium and the "Everyone else" list.

### How your score is calculated

```
Total = (videos completed × 10)
      + (your best quiz % ÷ 10, per quiz)
      + deadline points (on-time vs late)
      + assignment points
      + attendance points
```

Attendance points come from your **greytHR** punch records (≥95% of working days = full
points), imported monthly by your admin.

## 6. Weekly check-in

Once a week the dashboard shows a **check-in** prompt (about 90 seconds): three short
answers on what worked, what blocked you, and what's next. Keeping the streak going is
a quick way to build your Collaboration track.

> 📷 Screenshot: the weekly check-in form.

## 7. Growth Wizard & your trajectory

The **Growth Wizard** (5 minutes, once per cycle) sets your year up:

1. **Strengths** — pick what you're great at.
2. **Aspiration** — where you want to grow.
3. **Quests** — set three quarterly goals.
4. **Initiative** — pitch one bold idea.
5. **Review** — confirm and submit.

Your dashboard then tracks six areas — **Mastery, Delivery, Initiative, Collaboration,
Vision, Craft** — and rolls them into a **tier** (Stellar → Recalibrating). At year end,
the **Recap** gives you a Spotify-Wrapped-style summary.

> 📷 Screenshot: the Growth Wizard first step (Strengths).

---

## 8. Attendance & daily punch

Your dashboard has two quick links under **Attendance & daily punch**:

- **greytHR** — mark your daily attendance (punch in / punch out).
- **Turia** — your practice workspace.

Punch in/out on greytHR as usual; that attendance is what feeds your **Attendance points**
on the leaderboard each month.

---

## 9. Signing out

Click **Sign out** at the top-right of the dashboard. You can sign back in any time with
your Microsoft 365 account.

---

## FAQ

**I don't see any courses.** Your admin may still be setting things up, or no module is
assigned to you yet. Check back later.

**The quiz button is greyed out.** You need to watch ~90% of the video first. The button
says "Watch 90% to unlock" until then.

**My score is 0.** Scores build up as you complete videos and pass quizzes. Watch a video
and take its quiz to get started.

**Can I watch on my phone?** Yes — the portal works in any modern mobile browser.

**I changed my mind on a quiz answer mid-quiz.** You can change answers freely until you
submit. After submitting, use **Retake** if allowed.
